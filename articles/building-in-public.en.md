## Why Share?

For the longest time, I built things in silence. Code stayed in private repos. Ideas stayed in notebooks. And when projects failed, nobody knew — which meant nobody learned.

## The Turning Point

I stumbled across the concept of "building in public" from indie hackers and developers who shared their journey openly. At first it seemed counterintuitive. Why give away your secrets?

But the more I watched, the more I realized: **nobody cares about your "secret sauce."** They care about the journey. The struggles. The lessons.

## What I've Gained

- **Accountability** — When you announce you're building something, you have to ship it.
- **Feedback loops** — Sharing early means getting input before you've over-invested.
- **Connections** — The best opportunities have come from people who read my writing.

## How I Share

- Weekly updates on what I'm working on (and what's broken)
- Code snippets with explanations, not just final products
- Honest postmortems of failed approaches

## Try It Yourself

You don't need an audience. Start by sharing with one friend. Then a small group. Then the world.

The magic isn't in the sharing — it's in what happens *because* you shared.
