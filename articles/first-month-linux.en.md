## The Leap

After years of Windows development, I made the switch. No dual boot. No VM fallback. Just a format button and a USB stick with Ubuntu.

## Week 1: The Wall

![Linux desktop](iterator_cover.png)

Everything was hard. Package managers. Terminal workflows. The fact that my function keys didn't work out of the box.

I almost gave up on day 3.

## Week 2: Cracks in the Wall

Then something clicked. I wrote my first bash alias. I understood `grep` well enough to actually use it. I installed `tmux` and felt like a hacker in a movie.

## Week 3: Flow State

By week 3, I was faster than I ever was on Windows. The terminal became an extension of my thinking. `apt install` replaced 15 minutes of clicking through a GUI installer.

## What I Wish I Knew

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Missing drivers | `ubuntu-drivers autoinstall` |
| Fonts look bad | Install `fonts-noto` and enable hinting |
| Can't find apps | Learn `apt search` and `flatpak` |
| Terminal is ugly | Install `kitty` + `zsh` + `powerlevel10k` |

## Verdict

The first month is painful. The second month is productive. By the third month, you'll wonder why you waited so long.

Stop dual-booting. Take the leap.
